mFRR is the reserve Energinet activates each quarter to balance the grid. Up-regulation means the system is short of power, and the imbalance price usually rises above day-ahead; down-regulation means the system is long. The imbalance price is published about 45 minutes after the quarter ends, so the activation data is the earliest public sign of where it will settle.

Next 3 hours: what the model expects

Newest issue 06:02. The live model (V3) forecasts the gap between the imbalance price and the day-ahead price as a range, plus the chance of each regulation direction. Each quarter shows its newest forecast: the nearest quarters are 30–60 minutes ahead, the far ones up to 3 hours.
